What I do

Routine and specialized foot care for adults of all ages, with extra care for clients who find it hard to reach their own feet.

Routine nail care

Trimming, filing and shaping of thickened or hard-to-reach toenails.

Callus & corn care

Careful reduction of hardened skin and pressure points so walking feels easier.

Ingrown nail care

Relief and ongoing management of nails that curve into the skin.

Thickened & fungal nails

Reduction and tidying of thick, discoloured nails, plus advice on care between visits.

Diabetic foot care

Gentle care and routine checks for circulation, sensation and skin changes.

Education & prevention

Practical guidance on footwear, moisturizing and what to watch for.

How a visit works

Most appointments take about 45–60 minutes.

  1. Get in touch

    Call or send a message. We'll talk about what you need and find a time that suits you.

  2. I come to you

    I arrive with everything required, including sterilized instruments and a comfortable setup.

  3. Care and advice

    Treatment at your pace, plus simple suggestions for keeping your feet well between visits.

  4. Stay on schedule

    Many clients book a standing appointment every 6–8 weeks.
